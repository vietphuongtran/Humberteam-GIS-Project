Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our platform and data

Who is this platform for?

This platform is designed for educators, students, policy analysts, researchers, and anyone interested in understanding the landscape of international education in Canada. Whether you're an academic looking for research data, a student considering study abroad options, or a policymaker analyzing trends, our tools provide valuable insights.

Is there a cost to use this platform?

No, the platform is completely free to use! We believe in open access to educational data. The dashboard is open-source and available for public use. Our sponsors help maintain the platform so users never have to pay.

Where does the data come from?

We source our data from publicly available government databases (like Statistics Canada) and contributions from our sponsors such as York University and Humber College. All data is carefully vetted for accuracy before being included in our platform.

How often is the data updated?

Our data is updated annually with the latest available statistics from government sources. Some datasets may be updated more frequently when new information becomes available.

What time periods does the data cover?

Our historical data goes back to 2009 for most metrics, with more recent years having more detailed breakdowns. We're continuously working to add older historical data where available. The platform currently tracks trends up to the most recent available Statistics Canada data.

What technologies power this platform?

The platform is built with modern web technologies including HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript (with Vue.js framework), and Python for data processing. Our visualizations use D3.js and Chart.js, while the interactive maps are powered by Leaflet. The backend uses PostgreSQL for data storage. Graphs were designed in R. Maps were developed in QGIS.

Can I export the data or charts?

Absolutely. Users can download filtered datasets as CSV/Excel files for use in presentations and reports. Simply use the export buttons available on each visualization. Please remember to cite the source when using our data in publications.

How can I cite data from this platform?

We recommend the following citation format: "International Student Trends Dashboard [Year of data]. Retrieved from [URL] on [Date of access]." Each dataset page provides specific citation suggestions. For academic publications, please include the specific dataset version if available.

Can I request specific data analysis?

While we can't fulfill all individual requests, we welcome suggestions for new datasets or analyses that would benefit the community. Please use our Contact form to submit your ideas. For custom research projects, our team at York University may be available for consulting (fees may apply).

Who maintains this platform?

The platform is maintained by a team of developers and researchers from Humber College, with support from York University. Our interdisciplinary team includes data scientists, web developers, and education researchers working together to keep the platform updated and improve its features.

Is my data private when I use this platform?

We don't collect any personally identifiable information about our users. The platform only uses aggregate, anonymized educational data. Any usage statistics we collect are completely anonymous and used only to improve the service.

Are there limitations to the data?

While we strive for comprehensive coverage, some limitations include: data may not include all institutions, some historical data has gaps, some institutions may have missing geographic data, and certain demographic breakdowns may be limited to protect privacy.

Can I contribute data to the platform?

We welcome data contributions from qualified institutions and researchers. All contributed data goes through a verification process before being added to the platform. Please contact our data team to discuss potential contributions and review our data submission guidelines.
