About Our Platform

Built by students. Backed by data. Powered by collaboration.

Our Mission

Student Trends is a public-facing, interactive platform that visualizes key data about the presence and growth of postsecondary students in Canada. From top provinces to gender and education trends, the tool is designed for everyone—students, educators, researchers, and policymakers.

We believe that transparent access to educational data empowers better decision-making at all levels. Our goal is to make complex international student data understandable and actionable through intuitive visualizations.

Our Story

The platform was developed as a capstone initiative by students of Humber College using real open data and intuitive technologies. What began as an academic project has grown into a valuable resource for understanding global student mobility patterns.

With sponsorship and support from York University, the project promotes educational transparency and accessibility. Our team of student developers worked closely with academic advisors to ensure the platform meets the needs of diverse users.

Our Values

Transparency

We believe educational data should be freely accessible to all stakeholders.
